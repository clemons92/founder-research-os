Quantified data from June 2026: free accounts average <100 impressions/post; Standard Premium averages ~600; Premium+ averages ~1,550. That's a 10x reach difference. The algorithm gives Premium a documented +4 to +16 TweepCred boost. Additionally, X articles now make up 45% of top-performing posts — a format shift that favors long-form BiP content. If the X account for this project is not on Premium, the BiP distribution thesis is broken before it starts. Cost: $8/mo.
