## 4. Rubric Scoring

### 1. Pain / Willingness to Pay — 8/10

**Evidence:** Founders actively pay $5-199/mo for validation and competitive intelligence tools. IdeaProof (credit-based, EUR 19.99-99.99/mo), Spyglass ($79-199/mo for monitoring), WorthBuild ($5/report). The pain is real: 90% of indie hackers fail at distribution, not product — yet most tools focus on product validation, not ongoing market intelligence.

**Risk:** Must prove daily cadence adds enough value over one-shot. Mitigated by experiment generation (not just reports) and daily signal tracking (markets change fast).

### 2. Low CAC / Distribution — 8/10

**Evidence:** Build in Public is the proven indie distribution channel (4 months → $8K MRR launch). The product IS the marketing — daily research reports become shareable content. Reddit/IH/X communities are the exact audience. Every report is a potential content piece.

**Risk:** Requires consistent posting discipline. Mitigated by daily agent runs producing content automatically.

### 3. Railway + Lean Infra Fit — 9/10

**Evidence:** Railway is the 2026 default solo-founder hosting platform. Next.js + Railway + Supabase is the standard stack. Experiment generation (landing pages, fake doors) deploys natively on Railway. Agent runs are API-call-heavy — Railway handles cron jobs and background workers well. Costs stay under $15/mo until significant scale.

### 4. Agentic Differentiation — 8/10

**Evidence:** No competitor runs daily multi-source agent research grounded in verified cases. IdeaProof uses "a single AI call per report." Our approach: parallel agent simulation across web + X + IH, adversarial verification to kill hallucinations, one-click Railway-deployable experiments. The agent IS the product, not a wrapper around one API call.

**Risk:** Agent quality must demonstrably exceed one-shot tools. Mitigated by verified-case grounding and adversarial kill chain.

### 5. Speed to Validation / Revenue — 9/10

**Evidence:** MVP shippable in days (existing Next.js + Railway codebase). Beta pricing ($29-49/mo) with immediate value delivery (this report IS the first delivery). Fake-door landing page can go live today. Following Subscribr's pre-sell pattern.

### 6. Solo Feasibility — 9/10

**Evidence:** API-based architecture (LLM calls + web fetching), no complex infrastructure. Content doubles as marketing (zero additional marketing effort). Operating costs under $500/mo. Every verified case above was solo-founded at this stage. Marc Lou and Pieter Levels prove one person can run $1M+ businesses.

### 7. Competition Gap — 7/10

**Evidence:** Clear gap today — no tool combines daily research + verified cases + deployable experiments. But: the AI validation space is crowding fast (5+ tools launched in 2025-2026). Well-funded CI platforms ($38.6B market) could pivot down-market.

**Mitigation:** Speed + niche focus + community trust (BiP) create defensibility. Indie founders trust indie founders, not enterprise tools.

### 8. Alignment with Real Cases — 8/10

**Evidence:** Directly maps to Subscribr (niche AI tool for specific workflow, pre-sell to validate), Inbox Zero (open-source credibility + premium), Marc Lou (ship fast, portfolio, BiP distribution). The $10K MRR target is median achievable in 12-18 months for this pattern class.

### 9. Excitement / Sustainability — 7/10

**Evidence:** Daily research creates habit loop and retention. Content engine is self-feeding (agent outputs = marketing content). But: risk of "interesting but not urgent" — founders may consume reports without acting, leading to churn.

**Mitigation:** Experiment generation makes reports actionable. Track experiment deployment rate as leading indicator of stickiness.

### 10. Overall Risk — 7/10

| Risk Type | Level | Why |
|-----------|-------|-----|
| Technical | Low | API-based, standard stack, proven patterns |
| Market | Medium | Must prove daily > one-shot |
| Competition | Medium | Crowding space, but niche protects |
| Execution | Low | Solo-feasible, fast path to MVP |
| Financial | Low | Under $500/mo operating costs to start |

### TOTAL SCORE: 80/100 (7.8/10 weighted)
